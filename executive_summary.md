\# Executive Summary



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes sales performance using the Sample Superstore dataset. The analysis focuses on five key business areas: regional sales performance, top-selling products, category profitability, monthly sales trends, and the relationship between discount levels and average profit. SQL was used to analyze the data and answer the business questions, while Python, using Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, was used to visualize the results.



The objective of this project was to identify important patterns in sales and profitability and provide data-driven insights that can support business decision-making.



\## Key Findings



\### Regional Sales Performance



The West region generated the highest total sales at 713,471, while the South region generated the lowest total sales at 388,984. Based on total sales, the West was the strongest-performing region, while the South was the weakest-performing region.



\### Top-Selling Products



The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the top-selling product by total sales, generating 61,600. The analysis also identified the top 10 products with the highest total sales in the dataset.



\### Category Profitability



Technology generated the highest total profit at 145,387 and had the highest profit margin at 17.39%. Furniture generated the lowest total profit at 16,981 and had the lowest profit margin at 2.32%.



\### Monthly Sales Trends



Monthly sales fluctuated throughout the period analyzed. The highest monthly sales occurred in November 2017 at 117,383.38, while the lowest occurred in February 2014 at 4,119.82.



\### Discount and Average Profit



Average profit was positive at discount levels up to 20%, while discount levels of 30% and above were associated with negative average profit.



\## Business Recommendations



\- Investigate the factors contributing to the West region's stronger sales performance and identify opportunities to improve sales performance in the South region.

\- Monitor top-selling products to understand their contribution to overall sales performance.

\- Review the Furniture category to identify potential opportunities to improve its low total profit and profit margin.

\- Review discount levels of 30% and above because they were associated with negative average profit in the analysis.
